Algebra General Exam - August 2025

Problem 1

Let 𝔽q\mathbb{F}_{q} be a finite field with qq elements.

(a) Find the orders of the general linear group GL2(𝔽q)\mathrm{GL}_{2}(\mathbb{F}_{q}) and special linear group SL2(𝔽q)\mathrm{SL}_{2}(\mathbb{F}_{q}): (4 points) GL2(𝔽q):={A∈M2(𝔽q)∣det⁡A≠0},SL2(𝔽q):={A∈M2(𝔽q)∣det⁡A=1}.\mathrm{GL}_{2}(\mathbb{F}_{q}) := \{A \in \mathrm{M}_{2}(\mathbb{F}_{q}) \mid \det A \neq 0\}, \qquad \mathrm{SL}_{2}(\mathbb{F}_{q}) := \{A \in \mathrm{M}_{2}(\mathbb{F}_{q}) \mid \det A = 1\}.

(b) Show that SL2(𝔽q)\mathrm{SL}_{2}(\mathbb{F}_{q}) contains elements of orders q−1q-1 and q+1q+1. (Hint. To find an element of order q+1q+1, use the extension 𝔽q2/𝔽q\mathbb{F}_{q^{2}}/\mathbb{F}_{q}.) (6 points)

Problem 2

As usual, S6S_{6} and A6A_{6} will denote the symmetric and alternating groups on 6 elements.

(a) Let σ=(123)(456)∈A6\sigma = (123)(456) \in A_{6}. Find the sizes of the conjugacy classes CS6(σ)C_{S_{6}}(\sigma) and CA6(σ)C_{A_{6}}(\sigma). (5 points)

(b) Let τ=(12345)∈A6\tau = (12345) \in A_{6}. Find the sizes of the conjugacy classes CS6(τ)C_{S_{6}}(\tau) and CA6(τ)C_{A_{6}}(\tau). (5 points)

(a) Use the table to determine the decomposition of the representation ρ4⊗ρ5\rho_{4} \otimes \rho_{5} into the direct sum of irreducible representations, where ρi\rho_{i} denotes the irreducible representation of GG with character χi\chi_{i}. (5 points)

Problem 4

Let M=ℤnM = \mathbb{Z}^{n} be a free ℤ\mathbb{Z}-module of rank n≥2n \geq 2. We recall that the group SLn(ℤ)={A∈Mn(ℤ)∣det⁡A=1}\mathrm{SL}_{n}(\mathbb{Z}) = \{A \in \mathrm{M}_{n}(\mathbb{Z}) \mid \det A = 1\} acts on MM by automorphisms through matrix multiplication. Set X={(a1,…,an)∈M∖{0}∣g.c.d(a1,…,an)=1}.X = \{(a_{1}, \ldots, a_{n}) \in M \setminus \{0\} \mid \mathrm{g.c.d}(a_{1}, \ldots, a_{n}) = 1\}.

(a) Show that for any w∈Xw \in X there exists a basis v1,…,vnv_{1}, \ldots, v_{n} of MM such that v1=wv_{1} = w. (4 points)

(b) Let w=(6,10,15)w = (6, 10, 15). Find a basis v1,v2,v3v_{1}, v_{2}, v_{3} of M=ℤ3M = \mathbb{Z}^{3} such that v1=wv_{1} = w. (3 points)

(c) Use part (a) to show that SLn(ℤ)\mathrm{SL}_{n}(\mathbb{Z}) acts on XX transitively. (3 points)

Problem 5

Let FF be a field of characteristic 5. Classify up to conjugacy all (5×5)(5 \times 5)-matrices XX over FF satisfying X5=I5X^{5} = I_{5}. (10 points)

Problem 6

Let R=ℂ[x,y]R = \mathbb{C}[x, y] be the ring of complex polynomials in two variables. For an element a∈Ra \in R, we let (a)(a) denote the principal ideal generated by aa. Prove the following properties of the ring RR.

(a) While the g.c.d. of xx and yy is 1, show that the natural homomorphism R→R/(x)×R/(y)R \to R/(x) \times R/(y) is not surjective. (4 points)

(b) Prove that no principal ideal of RR is maximal. (6 points)

Problem 7

Let FF be a field, and let f(x),g(x)∈F[x]f(x), g(x) \in F[x] be nonconstant polynomials. Then the quotients F[x]/(f(x))F[x]/(f(x)) and F[x]/(g(x))F[x]/(g(x)) can be considered as F[x]F[x]-modules/algebras as well as FF-vector spaces/algebras.

(a) Use the universal property of tensor product to show that there exists a surjective homomorphism of FF-algebras (4 points): γ:F[x](f(x))⊗FF[x](g(x))→F[x](f(x))⊗F[x]F[x](g(x)).\gamma \colon \frac{F[x]}{(f(x))} \otimes_{F} \frac{F[x]}{(g(x))} \to \frac{F[x]}{(f(x))} \otimes_{F[x]} \frac{F[x]}{(g(x))}.

(b) Show that γ\gamma is an isomorphism if and only if ff and gg are proportional linear polynomials. (6 points)

Problem 8

Let E/KE/K be a cyclic Galois extension of degree nn with Galois group G=⟨σ⟩G = \langle \sigma \rangle. Show that there exists a∈Ea \in E such that a,σ(a),…,σn−1(a)a, \sigma(a), \ldots, \sigma^{n-1}(a) is a KK-basis of EE. (Hint. Consider σ\sigma as a KK-linear automorphism of the KK-vector space EE. Determine the minimal polynomial of σ\sigma, and use this information to conclude that EE has a “cyclic” basis for σ\sigma.) (10 points)
