Algebra, September 1978

  1. Prove that the set of rational 3×33 \times 3 matrices which commute with the matrix

[006104012]\left[\begin{array}{lll} 0 & 0 & 6 \\ 1 & 0 & 4 \\ 0 & 1 & 2 \end{array}\right]

is a field.
2. Let VV be a 12-dimensional vector space over the field GF(3)G F(3). Let TT be a linear transformation whose characteristic polynomial factors into linear factors over GF(3)G F(3). Find all possible Jordan normal forms given the table:

Transformation Rank

T3T^{3} 9

T−2T-2 9

(T−2)3(T-2)^{3} 6

  1. A certain group of order 60 is known to have exactly four members of order 5. Explain why they, along with the identity, form a normal subgroup.

  2. (a) Show that the Galois group over the rationals of every irreducible 5th degree polynomial contains an element of order 5 .
    (b) Show that the Galois group over the rationals of x4+1x^{4}+1 does not contain an element of order 4 .
    (c) Show that the Galois group over the rationals of x4+x3+1x^{4}+x^{3}+1 contains an element of order 4.

  3. Let MM be a module over an integral domain AA with quotient field KK. Show that MM is torsion free if and only if 0→M→M⊗A𝒦0 \rightarrow M \rightarrow M \otimes_{A} \mathcal{K} is exact.

  4. A local ring is defined to be a commutative ring with unit having a unique maximal ideal.
    (a) Prove a commutative ring, RR, is local if and only if the non-units form an ideal.
    (b) A domain, VV, is called a valuation domain if for a,b∈Va, b \in V either aa divides bb or bb divides aa. Show that a valuation domain is a local ring.
    (c) Prove that a local ring has no idempotents (other than 1).

  5. Let AA be a simple Artinian ring, and let NN be a minimal right ideal.
    (a) Viewing NN as a right AA-module, show that the ring D=End⁡A(N)D=\operatorname{End}_{A}(N) of AA-linear endomorphisms of NN is a division ring.
    (b) Prove AN=AA N=A.
    (c) Let kk be minimal such that there exist m1,…,mkm_{1}, \ldots, m_{k} in NN and a1,…,aka_{1}, \ldots, a_{k} in AA with 1=a1m1+⋯+akmk1=a_{1} m_{1}+\cdots+a_{k} m_{k}. Prove that the map

N⊕⋯⊕N⏟k− copies →A\underbrace{N \oplus \cdots \oplus N}_{k-\text { copies }} \rightarrow A

given by (x1,…,xk)→a1x1+⋯+akxk\left(x_{1}, \ldots, x_{k}\right) \rightarrow a_{1} x_{1}+\cdots+a_{k} x_{k} is an isomorphism of right AA-modules.
(d) Deduce A≅End⁡A(Nk)≃Mk(D)A \cong \operatorname{End}_{A}\left(N^{k}\right) \simeq M_{k}(D), the algebra of k×kk \times k matrices over DD. What famous theorem is this?
