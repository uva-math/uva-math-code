Algebra Comprehensive Exam

August, 1995

  1. Describe a nonabelian group of order 55 as semidirect product, and, using your description, explicitly give generators and defining relations for the group.

  2. Let ρ1:G→Aut⁡(V),ρ2:G→Aut⁡(W)\rho_{1}: G \rightarrow \operatorname{Aut}(V), \rho_{2}: G \rightarrow \operatorname{Aut}(W) be linear representations of a finite group GG. Let L:V→W\mathrm{L}: \mathrm{V} \rightarrow \mathrm{W} be a linear map. Show that the linear map L:V→W\mathrm{L}: \mathrm{V} \rightarrow \mathrm{W}, defined by

L′v=(1/|G|)∑g∈Gρ2(g−1)Lρ1(g)v,v in V,L^{\prime} v=\left({ }^{1 /|G|}\right) \sum_{g \in G} \rho_{2}\left(g^{-1}\right) L \rho_{1}(g) v, \quad v \text { in } V,

is an intertwiner of the representations ρ1\rho_{1} and ρ2\rho_{2}, i.e. L′ρ1(g)=ρ2(g)L′L^{\prime} \rho_{1}(g)=\rho_{2}(g) L^{\prime}, for all g∈Gg \in G.
3. Let GG be the Galois group of x4−4x^{4}-4 over the field QQ of rational numbers and let FF be the splitting field of x4−4x^{4}-4. Find GG and describe the action of each of its elements on FF. Also find all subfields of FF.
4. If TT is a diagonalizable linear operator on a finite dimensional vector space VV, and if WW is a TT-invariant subspace of VV, prove there is a TT-invariant subspace UU such that V=W⊕UV=W \oplus U.
5. Factor the polynomial x9−xx^{9}-x in F3[x]F_{3}[x] into irreducible factors. ( F3F_{3} is the field with three elements.)
6. Assume T is a linear operator on a 10 -dimensional real vector space V with minimum polynomial (x4−1)2\left(x^{4}-1\right)^{2}, and let kk denote the maximum number of linearly independent eigenvectors of T in V . What are the possible values of k ? Justify your answer.
7. Find the order of the group GL⁡(n,Fp)\operatorname{GL}\left(n, F_{p}\right), and describe a pp-Sylow subgroup. (p is a prime number, and Fp\mathrm{F}_{\mathrm{p}} is the field with p elements.)
8. A representation of the symmetric group S3S_{3} in the vector space 𝐑3\mathbf{R}^{3} is defined by letting a permutation in S3S_{3} act on a vector in 𝐑3\mathbf{R}^{3} by permuting the coordinates of the vector. Express 𝐑3\mathbf{R}^{3} as a direct sum of irreducible S3S_{3}-invariant subspaces. Prove that your direct summands are irreducible S3S_{3}-invariant subspaces.
9. List the conjugacy classes of finite subgroups of the orthogonal group O(2,𝐑)O(2, \mathbf{R}), and prove your list is correct.
10. The space Mn(R)M_{n}(R) of n×nn \times n real matrices is a real inner product space with inner product given by: ⟨A,B⟩=trace⁡(ABt)\langle A, B\rangle=\operatorname{trace}\left(A B^{t}\right), where BtB^{t} denotes the transpose of the matrix BB. (You don’t need to prove this.) Let PP be an invertible matrix and TT the linear operator on Mn(ℝ)M_{n}(\mathbb{R}) defined by T(A)=PtAPT(A) =\mathrm{P}^{\mathrm{t}} \mathrm{AP}. Denote the adjoint of T by T*\mathrm{T}^{*}. Prove that T*(A)=PAPt\mathrm{T}^{*}(\mathrm{~A})=\mathrm{PAP}^{\mathrm{t}}, and find necessary and sufficient conditions on the matrix P that T=T*\mathrm{T}=\mathrm{T}^{*}. Justify your answer.
