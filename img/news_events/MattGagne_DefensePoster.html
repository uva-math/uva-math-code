The family index of the odd signature operator with coefficients in a flat bundle

University of Virginia, Department of Mathematics

  • Speaker: Matt Gagne
  • Advisor: Nicholas Kuhn
  • Defense date: April 24, 2019
  • Location and time: Gilmer 166 at 1 pm

Abstract

The Atiyah-Singer index theorem provides an analytic interpretation of certain topological invariants associated to a MM-bundle f:E→Bf:E\to B, MM a smooth closed oriented Riemannian manifold. In particular a family of such operators D={Db}b∈BD=\{D_b\}_{b\in B} produces a class index⁡(D)∈K*(B)\operatorname{index}(D)\in K^*(B) in the complex K-theory of BB.

For example, the index of the (even) signature operator DeD^e coincides with the signature Sig⁡(M)\operatorname{Sig}(M) of the intersection form defined on the cohomology of MM, index⁡(De)=Sig⁡(M)=⟨ℒ(M),[M]⟩∈ℤ≅K0(pt.)\operatorname{index}(D^e)=\operatorname{Sig}(M)=\langle\mathcal L(M),[M]\rangle\in\mathbb Z\cong K^0(pt.), where ℒ(M)=ℒ(TM)\mathcal L(M)=\mathcal L(TM) is the total Hirzebruch L-class of MM.

Any elliptic operator DD on a manifold MM may be “twisted” by a vector bundle V→MV\to M, producing an operator DVD_V. When VV is flat index⁡(DVe)\operatorname{index}(D_V^e) again may be interpreted as the signature of an intersection form, this time defined on the cohomology H*(M;V)H^*(M;V) of MM with coefficients in VV,

index⁡(DVe)=Sig⁡V(M)=⟨ℒ(M)ch⁡([V]),[M]⟩∈ℤ≅K0(pt.).\operatorname{index}(D_V^e)=\operatorname{Sig}_V(M)=\langle\mathcal L(M)\operatorname{ch}([V]),[M]\rangle\in\mathbb Z\cong K^0(pt.).

Sig⁡V\operatorname{Sig}_V are the higher signatures, conjectured by S. Novikov to be invariants of the oriented homotopy type of MM.

Assume MM is odd dimensional. There is also an odd signature operator DoD^o and the family index of DoD^o is still related to the signature of EE. It was shown by J. Ebert that the family index of DoD^o is always zero, ind⁡(Do)=0∈K1(B)\operatorname{ind}(D^o)=0\in K^1(B), from which it follows that Sig⁡(E)=0\operatorname{Sig}(E)=0. Ebert’s proof, essentially functional analytic in nature, is still applicable to DVoD_V^o, so long as VV is flat Hermitian. Let V→EV\to E be a flat hermitian vector bundle.

Theorem. The the family index of the odd signature operator twisted by VV is zero, ind⁡(DVo)=0∈K1(B)\operatorname{ind}(D_V^o)=0\in K^1(B).

Let f!:H*(E)→H*−dim⁡(M)(B)f_!:H^*(E)\to H^{*-\dim(M)}(B) be the umkehr homomorphism (integration over the fiber), TvE=ker⁡(df)T_vE=\operatorname{ker}(df) the vertical tangent bundle of EE. Applying the Chern character ch⁡:K→Hℚ\operatorname{ch}:K\to H\mathbb Q yields the following vanishing results in rational cohomology.

Theorem. f!(ℒ(TvE)ch⁡([V]))=0∈H*(B;ℚ)f_!(\mathcal L(T_vE)\operatorname{ch}([V]))=0\in H^*(B;\mathbb Q).

Theorem. The higher signature Sig⁡V(E)=0\operatorname{Sig}_V(E)=0.

The special case when V=0V=0 was conjectured by Atiyah and proven by J. Ebert. This result generalizes an observation made by M. Gromov (when M=S1M=S^1). Note the flatness of VV above is essential. This can be seen in the simplest possible example where E=S1×S1→B=S1E=S^1\times S^1\to B=S^1 is the projection.
