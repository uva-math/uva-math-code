# “Mathemagics with Dr. Arthur Benjamin" The Mathematician and Magician - Sponsored by the Institute of Mathematical Science — poster text

The event information below is the text alternative to the original printed poster.

<p>Mathemagics with Dr. Arthur Benjamin, mathematician and magician.</p>

<p>March 23, 2015, 7:30pm. Physics Building 203, University of Virginia.</p>

<p>In his entertaining and fast-paced performance, Dr. Benjamin shows how to mentally add and multiply faster than a calculator, improve your memory for numbers, figure the day of the week for any day in history, and more.</p>

<p>More information: www.math.virginia.edu/magic. Admission is free. This talk is intended for a general audience.</p>

<p>Photo: Art Benjamin, Mathemagician (photo by Anne White, Hollywood, CA).</p>

<p>Sponsored by the Institute of Mathematical Science, University of Virginia, Charlottesville, Virginia. Contact: theims@virginia.edu.</p>
