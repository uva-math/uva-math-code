Submit New Course to CEPC

How to Submit Add/Edit Course to CEPC

See Course proposal submission guidelines

Download the CCI form and complete it. The form and other required documentation is sent to the College Registrar Ms. Judy Updike (jgu2k@virginia.edu).

A new course requires: a syllabus that contains a description, course requirements, and a weekly schedule of assigned work and activities.
