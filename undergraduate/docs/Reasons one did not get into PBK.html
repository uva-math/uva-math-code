Why Did I Not Get Into PBK?

Math and science majors with very high GPAs sometimes are not elected into Phi Beta Kappa, the national honor society. Selection considers a student's broader liberal arts education as well as academic achievement. Individual chapters determine their selection processes using national minimum criteria.

Consult the current Phi Beta Kappa membership stipulations. The national stipulations were updated on June 18, 2025.

Language study

Stipulation 2 states: “Candidates shall demonstrate knowledge of a second or non-native language at a college level.”

At UVA, the College's Phi Beta Kappa criteria also expect at least one intermediate foreign-language course (2020 level or higher) taken in residence at UVA, and at least one course in mathematics, logic, or statistics; see College awards and honors. This may influence the courses you select.

This page updates the department's guidance originally dated February 11, 2016. The older document referred to language study as Stipulation 3; students should use the current national stipulations when planning their courses.
