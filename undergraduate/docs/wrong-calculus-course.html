Suppose I Took MATH 1210 and Want to Be a Math Major

There is no easy answer when one takes the wrong first calculus course.

  1. Usually a student just takes MATH 1320 and works a little outside of class to make up the material not covered in MATH 1310.

  2. Another option to to sign up for MATH 1320 for credit and to Audit MATH 1310 to see what you missed. If you find that you are unprepared for MATH 1320 then drop it but continue with MATH 1310. You should then consider going to summer school to take MATH 1320 to get back on track.

Suppose I Took MATH 1220 and Want to Be a Math Major

To be a Math major you need to take MATH 2310 with a good grade, C or better. MATH 2310 requires material in MATH 1320.

  1. Usually a student just takes MATH 2310 and works a little outside of class to make up the material not covered in MATH 1220.

  2. Another option to to sign up for MATH 2310 for credit and to Audit MATH 1320 to see what you missed. If you find that you are unprepared for MATH 2310 then drop it but continue with MATH 1320. You should then consider going to summer school to take MATH 2310 to get back on track.
