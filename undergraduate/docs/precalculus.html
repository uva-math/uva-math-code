Suppose I need precalculus

Students who need a remedial review of algebra and trigonometry may elect MATH 1190 Applied Calculus I with Algebra which is a 4-credit hour course and includes a review of algebra and trigonometry. Credit is not allowed for both MATH 1190 and 1210 (or its equivalent).

Also Piedmont Virginia Community College offers Precalculus II MTH 164
Description : Presents trigonometry, analytic geometry, and sequences and series. Also includes conic sections, surface area and volume, and review of exponential and logarithmic functions. Prepares students for calculus with analytic geometry MTH 173. Prerequisite Satisfies V.P.T prerequisites for MTH 164 or MTH 270 or completion of MTH 163. Lecture 3 hours per week.
