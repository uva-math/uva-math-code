Weekly seminar email

This page reads every seminar calendar when it loads and prints one week in the layout of the weekly email. Copy the subject and the list into a new message to the seminar mailing list.

Check by hand before sending

  • Remove entries that are not department events. Everything on a calendar is printed, including an organizer's own notes (an exam, "no meeting", "Open").
  • Joint events sit on two calendars and are printed twice. Keep one.
  • Events on no seminar calendar are missing: add them by hand, or to the calendar first.
  • Where the speaker and the title were not separated, the calendar line is printed as it is.
