Unique continuation for elliptic equations with nonsmooth coefficients

Herbert Koch
March 24, 2000

Abstract

Let PP be a differential operator and let VV and WW be measurable functions. We consider sufficiently regular functions uu which satisfy

|Pu|≤V|u|+W|∇u||Pu|\le V|u|+W|\nabla u|

in a ball. We say the triple (P,V,W)(P,V,W) has the strong unique continuation property (SUCP) if uu vanishes if it vanishes of infinite order at one point.

Theorem (Koch, Tataru): Let n>2n>2 and let PP be an elliptic partial differential operator of second order with Lipschitz continuous coefficients. The triple (P,V,W)(P,V,W) has the SUCP if V∈Ln/2V\in L^{n/2} and W∈Ln+ϵW\in L^{n+\epsilon}.

The talk explains this result. It will give a sketch of the proof and explains its relation to questions occurring in the context of nonlinear dispersive equations.

The talk is based on joint work with D. Tataru.
