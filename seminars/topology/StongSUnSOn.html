Page 1: The manifolds and their cohomology

Recently, Peter Landweber wrote to ask some questions about the manifolds SU(n)/SO(n)SU(n)/SO(n). These are quite interesting manifolds and I thought I would tell you a bit about them. At the same time, I will include U(n)/O(n)U(n)/O(n).

To begin, one has fibrations SU(n)/SO(n)→iBSO(n)→⊗ℂBSU(n),SU(n)/SO(n)\xrightarrow{i}BSO(n)\xrightarrow{\otimes\mathbb C}BSU(n), U(n)/O(n)→iBO(n)→⊗ℂBU(n).U(n)/O(n)\xrightarrow{i}BO(n)\xrightarrow{\otimes\mathbb C}BU(n). If you consider the space of real nn-dimensional subspaces V⊂ℂnV\subset\mathbb C^n with the property that iV=V⟂iV=V^\perp is the orthogonal complement of VV, one has what is called the Lagrangian Grassmannian: Λn=U(n)/O(n).\Lambda_n=U(n)/O(n).
