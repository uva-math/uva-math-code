Transcribed from two-page handwritten notes. Download the printable transcript (PDF). The diagrams below are described in words as well as formulas.

Page 1: The poset and its realization

Let RkR^k denote the poset of proper, nontrivial linear subspaces of ℝk\mathbb{R}^k.

Examples. For k=2k=2, |R2|=ℝℙ1≃S1.|R^2|=\mathbb{RP}^1\simeq S^1. For k=3k=3, the realization is obtained from the space of flags between G1(ℝ3)G_1(\mathbb{R}^3) and G2(ℝ3)G_2(\mathbb{R}^3). The diagram is the pushout square with the flag space at the upper left, G1(ℝ3)G_1(\mathbb{R}^3) at the upper right, G2(ℝ3)G_2(\mathbb{R}^3) at the lower left, and |R3||R^3| at the lower right. The two maps from the flag space forget one of the two subspaces.

Goal. |Rk|=Sk(k+1)/2−2.|R^k|=S^{k(k+1)/2-2}.

Why look at this? The notes record Σ∞C(k,V)≃holim⁡poset⋁mS,\Sigma^\infty C(k,V)\simeq\operatorname{holim}_{\mathrm{poset}}\bigvee_m S, with C(k,V)C(k,V) labeled “configuration space.” This can be analyzed using orthogonal calculus: Σ+∞Mor⁡(ℝn,V)≃holim⁡0≠E≤ℝn(hom⁡(ℝn,V)−hom⁡((ℝn,E),(V,0))).\Sigma_+^\infty\operatorname{Mor}(\mathbb{R}^n,V) \simeq\operatorname{holim}_{0\ne E\leq\mathbb{R}^n} \bigl(\operatorname{hom}(\mathbb{R}^n,V)-\operatorname{hom}((\mathbb{R}^n,E),(V,0))\bigr). Here Mor\operatorname{Mor} is labeled “injective.” The next expression is ≃Nat⁡(|Rn↓−|,F),\simeq\operatorname{Nat}(|R^n\downarrow -|,F), and the general formula alongside it is holim⁡𝒞F≃Nat⁡(|𝒞↓−|,F(−)).\operatorname{holim}_{\mathcal C}F\simeq \operatorname{Nat}(|\mathcal C\downarrow -|,F(-)).

RkR^k is a category internal in Top\mathrm{Top}: space of objects=∐i=1k−1Gr⁡(i),\text{space of objects}=\coprod_{i=1}^{k-1}\operatorname{Gr}(i), space of morphisms=∐1≤i≤j≤k−1Flag⁡(i;j).\text{space of morphisms}=\coprod_{1\leq i\leq j\leq k-1}\operatorname{Flag}(i;j). The category diagram has source and target maps s,t:Mor⁡→Objs,t:\operatorname{Mor}\to\operatorname{Obj}, an identity map in the opposite direction, and a composition map Mor⁡×ObjMor⁡→Mor⁡.\operatorname{Mor}\times_{\operatorname{Obj}}\operatorname{Mor}\longrightarrow\operatorname{Mor}.

Form the simplicial nerve N•RkN_\bullet R^k. Its space NℓRkN_\ell R^k is the disjoint union of flags of length ℓ+1\ell+1, and |N•Rk|=:|Rk|.|N_\bullet R^k|=:|R^k|.

Let Symm⁡(k)\operatorname{Symm}(k) denote the space of k×kk\times k real symmetric matrices. Its dimension is k(k+1)/2k(k+1)/2.

Define f:N0Rk→Symm⁡(k)f:N_0R^k\longrightarrow\operatorname{Symm}(k) by sending EE to the orthogonal projection onto EE. With respect to E⊕E⟂E\oplus E^\perp, this is the matrix (1000).\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&0\end{pmatrix}. Extend ff linearly to a map |N•Rk|→Symm⁡(k).|N_\bullet R^k|\longrightarrow\operatorname{Symm}(k).

Page 2: Identification with a sphere

For p∈|Rk|p\in|R^k|, suppose pp is in the (d−1)(d-1)-simplex corresponding to the flag 0<E1<⋯<Ed<ℝk,0<E_1<\cdots<E_d<\mathbb{R}^k, with coordinates 0<τ1<⋯<τd−1<1.0<\tau_1<\cdots<\tau_{d-1}<1. Let τ0=0\tau_0=0 and τd=1\tau_d=1. Let Fi=Ei∩Ei−1⟂,F0=Ed⟂.F_i=E_i\cap E_{i-1}^{\perp},\qquad F_0=E_d^{\perp}. Then f(p)f(p) is the map that is multiplication by τi\tau_i on FiF_i.

Include ℝ\mathbb{R} into Symm⁡(k)\operatorname{Symm}(k) as the “constant diagonal” matrices. This acts on Symm⁡(k)\operatorname{Symm}(k) by addition, giving Symm⁡(k)/ℝ\operatorname{Symm}(k)/\mathbb{R}.

There is a nice representative for each equivalence class: 00 is the smallest eigenvalue. Since 0∈Symm⁡(k)/ℝ0\in\operatorname{Symm}(k)/\mathbb{R} has only one eigenvalue, f(p)≠0f(p)\ne0, so this descends to a map |Rk|→((Symm⁡(k)/ℝ)−{0})/ℝ>0≃Sk(k+1)/2−2.|R^k|\longrightarrow \bigl((\operatorname{Symm}(k)/\mathbb{R})-\{0\}\bigr)/\mathbb{R}_{>0} \simeq S^{k(k+1)/2-2}. Representatives of classes have all eigenvalues with least 00 and greatest 11.

Then it is visibly a bijection. The target is Hausdorff and the source compact, so it is a homeomorphism. For example, |R3|=S4.|R^3|=S^4.

Remark. Given a semisimple Lie group, there is a unique maximal compact, the quotient by which is a negatively curved symmetric space. Looking at flags of flat things embedded therein gives essentially the same idea.
