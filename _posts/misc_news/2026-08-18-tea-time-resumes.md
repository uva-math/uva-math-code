Math Department Tea Time resumes on **Tuesday, August 25, at 3:15 PM** in Kerchof 314 (Math Lounge).

On **Thursday, August 27, at 3:15 PM** we will hold a special welcome tea. Come meet our new colleagues and reconnect with everyone after the summer.

Tea is held every Tuesday and Thursday during the semester, starting at 3:15 PM. All faculty, staff, and students are welcome. Come to the 314 and enjoy connecting to the colleagues in the department over tea and light refreshments.
