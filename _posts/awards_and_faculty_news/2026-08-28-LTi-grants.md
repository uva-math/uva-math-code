Congratulations to [Evangelos Dimou]({{site.url}}/people/ed8bg) and [Josh Turner]({{site.url}}/people/rbh3vx) on receiving 2026-27 [Learning Technology Incubator (LTi) Grants](https://learningdesign.as.virginia.edu/grants) from A&S Learning Design & Technology! 

Evangelos Dimou's project is *Piloting PrairieLearn for Scalable, Digitally Accessible Math Assessment*, and Josh Turner's is *AI-Assisted Practice Problem Generation and Concept Explanation from LaTeX Course Materials*. Awardees will present the outcomes and future directions of their projects at a symposium in the spring.
