Professor {% include person_info_just_name.html UVA_id="vk6e" %} has used frontier AI models to make progress on a longstanding question in four-dimensional topology. In the preprint [*4-manifold topology and collision rank of groups*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25198), Krushkal reduces a problem about topological 4-manifolds to a problem in group theory and introduces the *collision rank* of a group. Working with AI models, he then found a large collection of new groups with the required properties, giving new examples of "good" groups, for which the standard tools of topological 4-manifold theory apply. A [new UVA Arts & Sciences article](https://as.virginia.edu/news/ai-transforms-mathematics-uva-researcher-sees-collaboration-key) describes the work and his view of AI as a collaborator in mathematical discovery. His continuing work is supported by a seed grant from the AI for Math Fund, sponsored by Renaissance Philanthropy and XTX Markets.
