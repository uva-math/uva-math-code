Congratulations to [Tai Melcher]({{site.url}}/people/tam7b) on being named a 2027 Fellow of the [Association for Women in Mathematics](https://awm-math.org/awards/awm-fellows/)! The AWM Fellows Program recognizes individuals who have demonstrated an outstanding, sustained commitment to the support and advancement of girls and women in the mathematical sciences.
