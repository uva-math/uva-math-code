[Lauren Williams](https://people.math.harvard.edu/~williams/) (Harvard University) delivered the [Virginia Mathematics Lectures]({{site.url}}/ims/lectures/lauren-williams/) on November 10 and 11, 2026, in Nau 101. Her first lecture, *Mathematicians Putting AI to the Test*, drew a large audience; the second was *Macdonald Polynomials and Probability*.

<!--more-->

The Virginia Mathematics Lectures are supported by the University of Virginia Department of Mathematics and the Institute of Mathematical Sciences.
