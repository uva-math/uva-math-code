The Department of Mathematics at the University of Virginia, Charlottesville, VA, invites applications for a Pitts Research Associate and Lecturer position. The successful candidates will be expected to conduct their own research, under supervision of a faculty mentor, and teach three courses per year. The appointment begins with the fall term of 2027, with an anticipated start date of August 25, 2027.

<!--more-->

All positions carry an initial appointment of one year, renewable for two additional years, contingent on satisfactory performance. The anticipated salary is $60,000.

## Qualifications

Applicants must be on track to receive a Ph.D. in Mathematics or a closely related field by June 2027 and must hold a Ph.D. at the time of appointment. Applicants must present evidence of outstanding accomplishments and promise in both research and teaching. All candidates whose research interests complement the strengths of the department's current faculty will be considered. Priority will be given to applicants in Functional Analysis, Operator Algebras and Geometric Topology. In addition, preference will be given to candidates who have received their Ph.D. within the last three years.

## Application Instructions

Apply online at [UVA Careers (R0088283)](https://jobs.virginia.edu/us/en/job/R0088283/Research-Associate-and-Lecturer-in-Mathematics). Also submit an application online at MathJobs (link forthcoming). A complete application must be completed in both systems and include the following documents: cover letter, curriculum vitae, a publication list, a description of research, and a statement about teaching interests and experience. At least four letters of recommendation, of which one must support the applicant's effectiveness as a teacher, must be submitted through MathJobs (link forthcoming).

The cover letter should briefly address

1. Areas of research interest;
2. Aims of future research;
3. Potential collaborations/projects at UVA;
4. Teaching experience.

Please note that multiple documents can be uploaded in the CV box.

Review of applications will begin November 15, 2026; however, the position will remain open until filled. The University will perform background checks on all new hires prior to employment. This position will also require an Education Verification (FSAKA).

For questions regarding the application process, please contact Rich Haverstrom, Academic Recruiter, at [rkh6j@virginia.edu](mailto:rkh6j@virginia.edu).

For additional information about the position, contact [math-hiring-postdoc@virginia.edu](mailto:math-hiring-postdoc@virginia.edu).

A competitive salary and benefits package is offered at the University of Virginia. For more information on the benefits available to postdoctoral associates at UVA, visit [postdoc.virginia.edu](https://postdoc.virginia.edu/) and [hr.virginia.edu/benefits](https://hr.virginia.edu/benefits).

UVA assists faculty spouses and partners seeking employment in the Charlottesville area. To learn more, please visit [dualcareer.virginia.edu](https://dualcareer.virginia.edu/).

For more information about UVA and the Charlottesville community, please see [virginia.edu/life/charlottesville](https://www.virginia.edu/life/charlottesville) and [embarkcva.com](https://embarkcva.com/).
