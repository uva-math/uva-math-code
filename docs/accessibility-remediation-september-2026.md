# UVA Mathematics accessibility remediation — September 2026

Implementation record for `UVA Math Website Accessibility Review September 2026.docx` in Downloads. All 24 embedded review screenshots were extracted and visually inspected. Changes apply to shared components and the public content inventory, including pages beyond the examples in the review.

## Scope and implementation

- Shared navigation, header, footer, page layouts and sidebars: visible focus, responsive navigation, correct landmarks and headings, readable supporting text, sufficient theme contrast, descriptive links, consistent social-link order and same-tab navigation.
- Home/news carousel: visible controls with generous targets, stop on hover and keyboard focus, reduced-motion handling, and inactive slides excluded from keyboard and assistive-technology navigation. News snippets expose one meaningful destination link.
- Local search and complete sitemap: content search includes department pages, people, news and document text; empty-result feedback and query handling are accessible. The error page offers useful routes back into the site.
- People, arXiv tracker, seminar calendars and archives: persistent labels, announced filter results, native disclosures, safe external calendar descriptions, visible loading/failure states and keyboard-operable controls. Fixed-width historical event tables became dated event sections. Current calendar text retains Eastern Time. The Seminars and Calendar pages no longer embed the Google Calendar week view; the accessible upcoming-talks list (now up to 100 talks) replaces it, followed by a link that opens every active seminar calendar in Google Calendar's agenda view.
- HTML documents: common site shell with stable original `.html` URLs; preserved MathML, document headings, figure descriptions, semantic exam numbering, table captions/headers, keyboard-scrollable wide equations and tables, and printable labeled forms. Native MathML supplies mathematical structure; raw TeX labels that overrode it were removed. Malformed nested MathML tokens were repaired without changing their text.
- Schedule: a generated, searchable HTML alternative contains all 44 courses and 129 sections, independent-study entries, evening exams, semester dates and the graduate weekly grid. The existing update workflow refreshes the HTML and validates a compact three-page tagged PDF automatically; both still reject room data.
- Posters, newsletters, programs and handouts: complete HTML companions, including the 42-speaker Selie program, historical seminar abstracts, handwritten topology notes, defense posters, conference programs and the 2020 graduation program. Source images and diagrams were inspected rather than relying only on extracted PDF text. Final newsletter review corrected 26 additional image descriptions, restored Jonathan Simone's missing portrait and placed Anna Pun and Charlotte Ure's portraits in their correct profiles. A later comparison with the original 2019 and 2021 newsletter PDFs restored photos the HTML issues had omitted and put each 2021 caption next to its photo. Exam review restored a table row and questions missing from the truncated April 1977, May 1978, September 1978, May 1980 and September 1980 algebra transcriptions, and added the September 1992 and January 1996 Part II analysis exams to the 1977–2004 archive. The MCLC introduction includes the recording's published English transcript.
- Renamed undergraduate documents: `undergraduate/docs/precalculus` and `wrong-calculus-course` replace the files whose names ended in four dots. The old dotless HTML URLs redirect to the new pages, and copies of the two PDFs remain at `If I need precalculus.pdf` and `If I took the wrong calculus course.pdf`.

See [the content matrix](accessibility-content-september-2026.md) for review-specific source paths and image work.

## Downloadable PDFs

The manifest inventories all 227 public PDF files, including files missed by an initial link-only crawl; the two PDF copies kept at the old undergraduate document names are duplicates of inventoried files. 174 PDFs are reflowed exports of complete HTML companions. Two more are tagged schedule outputs built and maintained by the schedule workflow, with their own report status. Reflowed document PDFs reproduce the reviewed HTML text, meaningful illustrations and equations; some obvious source typos were corrected in the companions, and the PBK note was updated to the current stipulations.

29 PDFs are original artwork tagged as one described Figure: the two poster-template assets and 27 one-page posters and flyers (18 IMS lecture posters, two Applied Mathematics Colloquium posters, the fluid dynamics conference poster and six PhD defense posters). The 29 poster PDFs replaced by text exports in the first pass were restored from the pre-remediation files, because their HTML alternatives link to them as the original poster. Tagging leaves their rendering unchanged. For six of them a non-visual correction came first: one inaccurate font `CIDSet` entry was removed, the default `Identity` `CIDToGIDMap` was written out for two TrueType CID fonts, and three posters had incomplete original tags replaced (one with its two links tagged). Follow-up repaired verified Computer Modern Unicode mappings in the Matt Gagne, William Craig and Badri Vishal Pandey defense posters; all three then passed PDF/UA-1 and rendered identically to their originals at 100 dpi. Each restored poster's complete text remains in its HTML alternative.

The exporter checks every formula and meaningful image description, supplies document language and title, creates PDF structure, converts preview links to public URLs, and runs veraPDF against PDF/UA-1. Blank table values appear as dashes in print. PDF equations have spoken alternatives; HTML retains navigable MathML. Combined exam archives include every constituent exam, not merely their table of contents.

The replacement process checks all candidates and original file hashes before changing any source PDF. The final machine-readable installation record is `accessibility-pdf-results-september-2026.json`: 203 replacements, 22 retained originals and two schedule outputs, with each retention category counted separately. Schedule builds refresh the corresponding PDF hashes and validation status in that record. ## Retained historical material and source limitations

- LP explicitly requested that the three Barry Simon decks remain public as historical archives. The same retention preference is applied to 15 additional historical lecture decks discovered in the full file inventory. These 18 originals total 1,929 pages and remain publicly available, with their accessibility limitations recorded in the manifest. They have not been fully remediated; retention is not a claim of accessibility or a legal exemption.
- Both copies of the example `PosterTemplate.pdf` remain untagged originals. Their embedded TeX arrow-tip font fragments lack usable Unicode mappings; assigning ordinary arrow characters to those fragments would misrepresent the content. `graduate/docs/PosterTemplate.html` describes the template. The three defense posters previously listed with this limitation have been repaired and validated as described above.
- The fillable `undergraduate/docs/Grader_App_2023.pdf` (25 form fields) and `undergraduate/docs/exception(fillable)_1.pdf` (24 fields) were restored unchanged, so they can again be filled in electronically. They are not tagged: PDF/UA would need structure for every field and tagged page text, and the exception form's checkbox appearances use a ZapfDingbats font that is not embedded. The labeled HTML forms, with their print action, are the accessible versions.
- The 1993 real-analysis exam's question 4 lacks its characteristic-function curve in both the original PDF (combined archive, page 43) and the existing image. The accessible description reports the axis points and the separate raised point labeled φ(λ) that the original PDF shows. A complete original is required to recover the missing mathematical data.
- Graduation recordings and other external services remain under their owners' control. Descriptive links and the MCLC transcript address the website presentation; they do not certify every external recording, registration form or calendar service.
- Original scanned notes contain occasional tentative statements or source typos. Companions identify uncertainty or explicit transcription corrections rather than silently inventing missing mathematics.

## Verification and release status

Local verification on September 13 and 14, 2026:

- Structural audit: 1,235 pages, zero findings. Checks cover language/titles, landmarks, heading order, links, images, lists, disclosures, form labels and native MathML.
- Browser coverage: a full 1,225-page crawl at 320px in dark mode passed with zero axe findings, horizontal page overflow or JavaScript errors. The subsequent 42 new/changed pages and five final content corrections passed at both 320px/dark and 1280px/light. Earlier desktop and focused component checks covered the shared layouts, forms, code and equation scrolling.
- Local references: the September 14 case-sensitive build check examined 101,199 local references across 1,280 HTML files including redirects. Its nine missing references are the same pre-existing `/mrbs/` targets; comparison with the earlier build found no new broken links.
- Interaction fixtures: keyboard navigation, carousel pause/focus/inactive slides, filters, search feedback, arXiv validation, calendar loading/error handling and mathematical descriptions passed. Calendar date tests covered Eastern-midnight events viewed in New York, Istanbul and Honolulu.
- PDF catalog: the September 13 pass validated every document export and both template artwork assets against veraPDF PDF/UA-1, and checked text bounds and embedded links across 209 candidates (1,126 pages) with no text outside a page and no local-preview or filesystem links. Visual samples included dense mathematics, figures, newsletters, programs, forms and all three schedule pages.
- PDF follow-up on September 14: 49 documents whose HTML sources changed after review (six newsletters, 35 graduate exam PDFs including the three combined archives, the PBK note, the course guide, the topology syllabus, the DRP mentor guidelines, two seminar abstracts, the fluid dynamics conference schedule and the 2020 workshop poster) were re-exported from a fresh build. The installed reflowed exports now carry 20,009 formula alternatives and 314 meaningful illustrations.
- Schedule generation: all 129 section rows, 44 headings, 14 supplementary paragraphs and seven weekly-grid rows were checked against the printed text. The complete update helper passed in an isolated directory and produced matching current/term PDFs and the expected HTML.
- JavaScript, Python and Ruby syntax checks and `git diff --check` passed. Four regression fixtures verify that the legacy conversion helper preserves native MathML and does not reintroduce raw-TeX labels.
- Final September 14 follow-up: the structural audit again found zero issues across 1,235 pages. All 67 pages containing tables passed at 320px/dark, 400px/light and 1280px/light with no mid-word splits or horizontal page overflow; keyboard scrolling was verified on the schedule and a historical algebra seminar archive. Updated calendar fixtures passed with event wording for the DRP and AMS calendars. Three additional defense posters passed PDF/UA-1 with unchanged 100-dpi rendering.
