## PDF and poster alternatives

The remediation now includes reflowed, tagged PDF exports from complete HTML companions, with separate validation before replacement. The three combined exam PDFs are assembled from every constituent exam, not just their HTML index. Original lecture slides remain public as historical archives under LP’s retention instruction. See `accessibility-remediation-september-2026.md` and `scripts/accessibility/pdf-manifest.json` for the final export status and limits. Shared document layout migration brings the HTML documents into the department navigation.

Eighteen new text alternatives under `IMS/poster-text/` reproduce the event information from the local poster PDFs, including schedules, lecture titles, affiliations, organizers, sponsors and any printed abstracts. They have matching `/img/IMS/*.html` public URLs and are linked from the corresponding post bodies. Text was extracted from the local PDFs and reviewed; the two multi-column schedules were put in logical reading order. The alternatives cover:

- Arthur Benjamin, Yair Minsky, Irene Fonseca, Barry Simon, Jacob Sherson and Andrei Okounkov.
- Van Vu, Peter Sarnak, Diane Hoffoss, Gregory Lawler and Curtis McMullen.
- Martin Hairer, Bjorn Poonen, Kavita Ramanan, Alan Reid, Jordan Ellenberg, Ravi Vakil and Richard Schwartz.

The Riverside Workshop post already includes its speakers, organizers and support in HTML; it now also exposes the website address printed on the poster as a normal link. Its poster says May 9–11, 2025, while the pre-existing announcement says May 9–12. That historical discrepancy was not silently resolved.

## Verification and remaining limits

- The six original placement exam variants were parsed and each contains ten top-level question list items. The 1210/1220 source had question 4 embedded in question 3's paragraph; it was split explicitly before checking the count.
- All flagged 2021 newsletter originals, both lab photographs, all six solution figures, the placement graph and road layout, all twenty graduate-exam diagrams, the ALEKS report and both maps were visually inspected before their descriptions were edited.
- Full site builds, automated accessibility scans and browser keyboard checks are run by the main task. This content matrix is not a blanket WCAG certification or a claim that every external destination is accessible.
- The MCLC introduction has a transcript from the recording’s published human English captions. Graduation recordings retain descriptive external links; their captions/transcripts remain with the video owners. No missing transcript was invented.
- PDF exports have machine-checkable structure, spoken formula alternatives and preserved meaningful image descriptions. Automated PDF/UA checks do not establish mathematical transcription accuracy, reading-order usability or blanket WCAG conformance. Historical slide decks remain explicitly unresolved.
- `graduate/exams/analysis/analysis-1993-08-real.html`, question 4, has a pre-existing incomplete characteristic-function figure. The image contains an axis and two points but no function curve; the associated `graduate/exams/analysis/1977-2004.pdf`, page 43, also lacks the curve. The description reports the visible information only. A complete original source is needed to restore the missing mathematical data.
- Historical event affiliations, dates and policies were otherwise preserved. The external links named in the report were corrected where a verified replacement or an existing local equivalent was available; this is not a claim that every decades-old external archive URL was revalidated.
