Schur dualities arising from quantum symmetric pairs

University of Virginia, Department of Mathematics

  • Speaker: Yaolong Shen
  • Advisor: Weiqiang Wang
  • Defense date: April 1, 2024, 1:30–3:00 pm
  • Location: Kerchof Hall 111
  • Homepage: Yaolong Shen

Abstract

A symmetric pair (𝔤,𝔤θ)(\mathfrak g,\mathfrak g^\theta) consists of a Lie algebra 𝔤\mathfrak g and a fixed point subalgebra 𝔤θ\mathfrak g^\theta, where θ\theta is an involution on 𝔤\mathfrak g. The corresponding quantum symmetric pair (𝑼,𝑼ı)(\mathbf U,\mathbf U^\imath), introduced by Letzter for finite type and Kolb for Kac-Moody type, is a qq-deformation of (𝔤,𝔤θ)(\mathfrak g,\mathfrak g^\theta). Here 𝑼\mathbf U is the quantum group associated to 𝔤\mathfrak g and 𝑼ı\mathbf U^\imath is a coideal subalgebra of 𝑼\mathbf U. The algebra 𝑼ı\mathbf U^\imath, now referred as the ıquantum group, can be viewed as natural generalizations of quantum groups.

The Schur-Jimbo duality is one of the most fundamental topics in representation theory, bridging the irreducible representations of a Hecke algebra with those of a Drinfeld-Jimbo quantum group. In 1, the authors establish an ıSchur duality where the Hecke algebra of type B, and the ıquantum group of quasi-split type AIII satisfy a double centralizer property. Furthermore, it was shown that the Kazhdan-Lusztig basis of type B coincides with the ıcanonical bases arising from tensor product modules of ıquantum groups.

We study ıquantum groups of AIII in a more general sense and establish an ıSchur duality which can be viewed as a common generalization of Jimbo-Schur duality and Bao-Wang’s quasi-split ıSchur duality. The ıcanonical bases in this setting provides a common generalization of both type A and type B Kazhdan-Lusztig bases.

Theorem (2). Let (𝑼,𝑼ı)(\mathbf U,\mathbf U^\imath) be a quantum symmetric pair of type AIII. The ıquantum group 𝑼ı\mathbf U^\imath and the Hecke algebra of type B form a double centralizer property on a tensor module of 𝑼\mathbf U.

The exploration of ıSchur dualities within the framework of quantum symmetric pairs provides a natural extension of classical Schur dualities. Specifically, the type AI ıquantum group serves as a quantization of the special orthogonal Lie algebra, while the type AII ıquantum group serves as a quantization of the spin Lie algebra. These classical limits form Schur dualities with the Brauer algebra.

Theorem (3). Let (𝑼,𝑼ı)(\mathbf U,\mathbf U^\imath) be a quantum symmetric pair of super type AI or AII. The ıquantum group 𝑼ı\mathbf U^\imath and the qq-Brauer algebra form a double centralizer property on a tensor module of 𝑼\mathbf U.

Motivated by the immense success of quantum symmetric pairs, we introduce quantum supersymmetric pairs associated with Lie superalgebras in 4 and establish their fundamental properties. Furthermore, we extend the aforementioned ıSchur dualities to the super setting.
